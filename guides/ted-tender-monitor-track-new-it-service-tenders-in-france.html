Track new IT service tenders in France — with EU Public Tender Monitor (TED)

Sales teams targeting French public sector buyers need a reliable stream of newly published IT service contracts. The data covers notices from the official EU procurement database, filtered to France and the IT services classification, so only relevant opportunities appear.

The feed answers the question of which tenders have been published or updated since the last check, giving a current view of open competitions, deadlines, and buying organisations. Each record includes the notice identifier, publication date, buyer name, procedure type, deadline, and a direct link to the full tender document.

Use the notice identifier to deduplicate records in a CRM, the publication date to sort by recency, and the buyer field to research the organisation. The CPV codes confirm the service category, while the procedure and deadline indicate the response timeline. Estimated value and currency, when present, help prioritise high‑value bids, and the URL provides immediate access to the official notice.

Configure the monitor by setting the country code to FRA, the CPV prefix to 72 for IT services, and an optional keyword such as "software" or "cloud" to narrow results further. The published‑since parameter ensures only new or changed notices are returned, avoiding duplicate processing.

The source is the EU TED API, which publishes official procurement notices daily. The product returns only incremental changes, so each run delivers a concise set of actionable leads without reprocessing the entire archive.
